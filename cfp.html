AOE@WSDM 2027

Call for Papers

Research, systems, benchmarks, datasets, and positions for reliable agent-in-the-loop optimization in e-commerce.

About the workshop

AOE examines e-commerce AI as a coupled system: agents support consumers and businesses, agents improve models and pipelines, and agent actions reshape the environments and evidence used for future optimization.

We invite work that advances shared abstractions, evaluation protocols, infrastructure, and design principles for self-improving e-commerce systems that remain empirically grounded, human-directed, and operationally accountable.

Topics of interest

Interaction Loop — agents working with e-commerce participants

  • Shopping, product-search, recommendation, comparison, negotiation, and decision-making agents.
  • Merchant, advertiser, campaign-management, creator, customer-service, and operations agents.
  • Goal and preference elicitation under ambiguity, sparsity, conflict, and drift.
  • Personalized memory, profile construction, provenance, editing, deletion, and privacy.
  • Tool use, multi-step planning, mixed-initiative interaction, and human delegation.
  • Multi-agent coordination, trust, explanations, consent, and human override.

System Optimization Loop — agents improving e-commerce AI systems

  • Auto-research: problem discovery, hypothesis generation, literature and code understanding, and experiment design.
  • Agent harnesses connecting data, code, training, evaluation, deployment, and monitoring tools.
  • Feature engineering, architecture search, bidding, budget pacing, campaign and policy optimization, debugging, and root-cause analysis.
  • Persistent research memory, experience replay, skill libraries, and transfer across tasks or domains.
  • Self-reflection, self-correction, self-evolution, and continual improvement under resource constraints.
  • Verification, reproducibility, rollback, attribution, audit trails, approval gates, and safe exploration.

Agent–Environment Loop — stateful environments for learning and evaluation

  • Sandboxed environments with executable search, recommendation, advertising, auction, transaction, and service tools.
  • User, merchant, creator, and marketplace simulation; language world models; and digital twins.
  • Stateful catalogs, inventory, prices, promotions, auctions, budgets, exposure, orders, fulfillment, returns, and policies.
  • Simulator calibration, behavioral fidelity, reproducibility, controllability, and sim-to-real validation.
  • Counterfactual interventions, rare-event generation, stress testing, and safe exploration.
  • Long-horizon behavior, multi-stakeholder value, robustness, fairness, privacy, manipulation, and reward hacking.

Submission categories

Up to 8 pages

Research / System Papers

Original methods, deployed systems, empirical studies, datasets, benchmarks, and evaluation protocols.

Up to 4 pages

Position / Demo Papers

Forward-looking perspectives, negative results, work-in-progress systems, and live demonstration proposals.

Page limits exclude references. Submissions should use the ACM sigconf format. Reviewing will be single-blind, and each submission will receive at least two reviews assessing relevance, technical quality, clarity, and discussion value.

In-person participation: at least one author of each accepted paper must register, attend, and present in Hong Kong. Remote presentations will not be supported.

Evaluation and reproducibility

We encourage submissions to report agent trajectories, intervention and compute budgets, simulator assumptions, human checkpoints, failed trials, and rollback decisions. Autonomous-system papers should make validation and reproducibility choices explicit.
