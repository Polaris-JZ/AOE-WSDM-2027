Read the call for papers View the program

E-commerce AI is moving beyond static prediction pipelines toward closed-loop systems in which agents interpret goals, use tools, retain experience, conduct experiments, and adapt decisions over time.

AOE connects two directions that are often studied apart: agents that assist consumers, merchants, advertisers, and operators; and agents that automate research, evaluation, deployment, and system improvement.

The research agenda is organized around three coupled loops: interaction, system optimization, and agent–environment.

The workshop brings together researchers and practitioners across information retrieval, recommender systems, computational advertising, multi-agent systems, human–AI interaction, and industrial AI.

Central question
How can e-commerce systems optimize through agents when every intervention changes the environment and the evidence?

At a glance

What belongs at AOE

We welcome empirical, theoretical, systems, benchmark, dataset, and position work across the full agent-in-the-loop lifecycle.

01 / PARTICIPANTS

Agents working with people and businesses

Shopping and decision agents; merchant, advertiser, campaign, creator, service, and operations agents; preference elicitation; delegation; trust and consent.

02 / SYSTEMS

Agents improving e-commerce AI

Auto-research, experiment harnesses, architecture and policy optimization, debugging, reproducibility, verification, approval gates, and rollback.

03 / ENVIRONMENTS

Stateful environments for learning and evaluation

Sandboxes, world models, simulations, counterfactuals, rare-event testing, multi-stakeholder value, robustness, fairness, and safe exploration.
