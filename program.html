Participation

Designed for discussion

At least one-third of each invited-talk slot is reserved for discussion. Lightning talks flow directly into posters and live demonstrations, creating time for focused exchange between academic and industrial participants.

A physical “three-loop map” will collect problems, datasets, environments, and open challenges throughout the session.
